# Questions for the University of Winchester

These go with the course page options prototype. Course details in the prototype were taken from winchester.ac.uk on 17 November 2026. Anything highlighted **To confirm** in the prototype is listed here, along with the suggestions we'd like you to agree.

## Course data and the CRM

1. Which course details does the CRM hold for each course? For example: UCAS codes, routes (such as with foundation year), placement years, modes of study, start dates, fees by fee status, typical offers and course lengths.
2. Does the CRM treat a foundation year route as a separate course record, as the website does (for example NP29 and NP2X)?
3. Does the CRM hold a placement year as part of the same course record, or separately?
4. Which fee statuses does the CRM use? The website shows "UK / Channel Islands / Isle of Man / Republic of Ireland" against "International" for most courses, and three columns for research degrees.
5. Where do Clearing status and Clearing entry requirements come from, and how are they switched on and off?
6. Do the apply links (UCAS, Oxford International, the postgraduate and research application pages, the online store for short courses) come from the CRM or are they entered by editors?

## Undergraduate (example: BA (Hons) Business Management)

7. What is the course length and end date for a January start with a placement year?
8. What is the fee for the placement year?
9. What is the international fee for the foundation year?
10. Can international applicants apply through Clearing, and if so, how?
11. Which intake does "Clearing 2026" apply to, and what are its start date and fees?

## Postgraduate taught (example: MSc Digital Marketing and Analytics)

12. Can applicants study for a PgDip or PgCert on its own on this course? If so, what are the length, credits, modules and fees for each?
13. What is the part-time fee?
14. Can international students study part-time?

## Research degrees (MPhil/PhD Research)

15. What are the maximum course lengths? The key facts say 4 years full-time and 7 part-time, but the fees table says 3 and 6.
16. What is the fee for Channel Islands and Isle of Man students? The fees table has a column for it, but no figure.

## Degree apprenticeships (example: MA (Hons) Social Work (Apprenticeship))

17. What are the start dates? The key facts say September, but the course details say January and September.

## Proposals to agree

These are suggestions in the prototype that differ from the current website:

- Showing only the content that matches the chosen options, instead of every version at once:
  - opening the matching tab in entry requirements
  - showing only the relevant fees
  - hiding the Pathways Programme for international applicants
  - showing "Information for International Students" to international applicants only
- Showing the placement year in the undergraduate course modules.
- Discover Uni statistics and the "Compare related courses" cards matching the chosen route.
- An "I am an employer / I am an apprentice" switch on apprenticeship pages, in place of showing both cost sections.
- Offering PgDip and PgCert as options on taught postgraduate pages.
- Passing the chosen start date through to the short course booking link.
